14th-17th Century (Transition from Medieval to Modern)

三大主要特徵 Three Main Features:

  • (i) 復興古希羅文明 Revival of Ancient Greco-Roman Civilisation:

    歐洲人熱衷研究古希臘及羅馬的遺產，在藝術及建築上重新採用古典風格，並親身驗證古代科學理論。
    Europeans became enthusiastic about studying Greco-Roman heritage, adopting classical styles in art and architecture, and testing ancient scientific theories.

  • (ii) 強調人文主義 Emphasising Humanism:

    將「人」置於關注的中心。強調表現人性、人類價值與理解世界的能力，堅決否定教會將「苦行」視為理想生活的觀念。
    Placed humans at the centre. Emphasised human nature, human values, and the ability to understand the world, rejecting the Church's view of suffering as ideal.

  • (iii) 挑戰傳統 Challenge to Tradition:

    高度重視理性思考和探索精神，不再盲目相信權威，主動質疑傳統及教會教導，積極追求學術突破與創新。
    Valued rational thinking and the spirit of inquiry. Refused to blindly follow authority, questioned traditions and Church teachings, and actively pursued breakthroughs.

成因 1 Cause 1

封建社會瓦解 Decline of Feudal Society

城鎮興起與黑死病爆發削弱了封建制度。農奴離開莊園成為自由市民，擁有了更多自由與時間投入藝術與學術。
The rise of towns and the Black Death weakened feudalism. Serfs left manors to become free townspeople with time for art and learning.

成因 2 Cause 2

古希羅與伊斯蘭文明刺激 Classical & Islamic Influences

阿拉伯人保留並翻譯了大量的古希羅典籍。11-13世紀的十字軍東征促進了歐亞知識交流，重新燃起歐洲人對古典文化的興趣。
Arabs preserved Greco-Roman works. The Crusades promoted knowledge exchange, reigniting interest in classical culture.

成因 3 Cause 3

富有城邦與新興階層 Rise of Wealthy City-States

意大利城邦因地中海貿易而繁榮。富裕的商人與銀行家追求世俗生活，大量慷慨資助藝術家與學者，以提升家族地位。
Italian city-states thrived on trade. Merchants and bankers pursued secular life, patronising artists to boost family status.

成因 4 Cause 4

大學興起與學者西逃 Rise of Universities & Scholars

12世紀起大學興起提供學術自由；14世紀拜占庭帝國動盪，學者攜帶珍貴古希羅手稿逃往意大利，保存在大學與圖書館中。
Universities provided academic freedom; Byzantine scholars fled to Italy with classical manuscripts stored in universities.

成因 5 Cause 5

教會權威下降 Decline of Church Authority

歐洲人發現教會教義有許多錯誤，十字軍東征的失敗亦削弱了教會威信。君主拒絕繳稅，促使人們擺脫宗教控制，探索人的自我價值。
Errors in Church teachings and failed Crusades reduced Church prestige. Monarchs refused Church taxes, encouraging people to break free from religious control.

文學發展 Literature

核心改變 Core Shift: 從中古時代「以宗教聖人傳記為主、使用拉丁文（Latin）」轉變為「以人為中心、展現世俗生活、使用各國母語（Vernacular Languages）」創作。
Shifted from religious biographies in Latin to human-centred, secular works written in native vernacular languages.

但丁 Dante (1265-1321)
作品 Work: 《神曲》 Divine Comedy (意大利文)
特色 Feature: 描繪天堂地獄，揭露社會不公。
Depicted heaven and hell, exposing social injustice.
薄伽丘 Boccaccio (1313-1375)
作品 Work: 《十日談》 Decameron (意大利文)
特色 Feature: 描述避疫故事，批判教會與展現人性。
Tales during plague, criticizing Church and human nature.
莎士比亞 Shakespeare (1564-1616)
作品 Work: 《哈姆雷特》 Hamlet 等 (英文)
特色 Feature: 深入探索複雜的人類情感與世俗現實。
Explored complex human emotions and secular reality.

藝術與雕塑 Art & Sculpture

藝術突破 Art Innovations: 引入透視法 (perspective)、光影明暗對比 (shadowing) 及人體解剖學 (dissection)，呈現立體深度 (3D)、精準比例 (proportional balance) 與極致的寫實感 (realism)。
Introduced perspective, shadowing, and human anatomy for 3D depth, precise proportional balance, and extreme realism.

藝術三傑 Three Great Masters of Art:
  • 達文西 Leonardo da Vinci: 精通透視與光影對比。代表作：《蒙娜麗莎》(Mona Lisa)、《最後的晚餐》(The Last Supper)。
    Mastered perspective and lighting shadow contrast. Famous works: Mona Lisa, The Last Supper.
  • 米開朗基羅 Michelangelo: 擅長展現人體肌肉動態與深層情感。代表作：西斯汀教堂天花畫 (Sistine Chapel ceiling)、《大衛像》(David)。
    Famous for muscle dynamics and profound human emotions. Works: Sistine Chapel ceiling, David.
  • 拉斐爾 Raphael: 作品以和諧、優雅與平靜著稱。代表作：《雅典學院》(School of Athens)。
    Known for harmony, elegance, and peace. Famous work: The School of Athens.

建築風格 Architecture

中古哥德式 Gothic Style (Before)
  • 高尖塔 Tall spires (象徵接近上帝 Symbolising being closer to God)
  • 尖形拱門 Pointed arches (引導視線向上 Guiding eyes upward)
  • 飛扶壁 Flying buttresses (支撐高大外牆 Supporting exterior walls)
  • 彩色玻璃窗 Stained-glass windows (講述聖經故事 Depicting Bible stories)

  • 對稱與比例 Symmetry & proportion (展現理性和諧 Displaying rational harmony)
  • 圓柱與三角楣 Columns & triangular pediments (古典希羅元素 Classical elements)
  • 半圓拱門與圓頂 Semi-circular arches & domes (優雅幾何美感 Geometric elegance)
  • 柱廊與對稱廣場 Colonnades & symmetrical squares (人本開放空間 Human-centered space)

自然科學 Natural Science

醫學 Discoveries in Medicine

突破解剖禁令 Broke dissection bans:

  • Fracastoro (弗萊卡斯楚): 證實疾病由細菌傳播。
    Proved that diseases are spread by germs/bacteria.
  • Vesalius (維塞利亞斯): 親自解剖人體，詳細紀錄骨骼與器官結構。
    Dissected human bodies, mapping bones and organ structures in detail.
天文學 Astronomy

推翻地心說 Overthrew Geocentrism:

  • Copernicus (哥白尼): 提出「日心說」(Heliocentrism)。
    Proposed Heliocentrism: the Sun is the centre of the universe.
  • Galileo (伽利略): 用自製望遠鏡觀測實證日心說。
    Proved Heliocentrism empirically using a telescope.
1. 質疑拉丁聖經 Doubts on Latin Bible

埃拉斯默斯 (Erasmus) 出版希臘文新約聖經，揭露拉丁文譯本有誤，許多教會教義缺乏聖經依據。
Erasmus published Greek New Testament, revealing errors in Latin translation and unbiblical doctrines.

2. 君主不滿 Resentment of Kings

教會干預世俗政治，強大的歐洲君主希望削弱教會勢力以擴張王權與稅收。
Church interfered in politics; powerful European monarchs sought to reduce Church power and retain taxes.

3. 教會腐敗 Church Corruption

聖職人員販賣神職與贖罪券 (indulgences)，宣稱購買後可減少在煉獄 (purgatory) 中的受苦時間。
Clergy sold church offices and indulgences, claiming to reduce punishment in purgatory.

導火線 Immediate Trigger

1517年販賣贖罪券與《九十五點論證》(Ninety-Five Theses)

天主教會為籌款興建羅馬聖伯多祿大教堂 (St. Peter's Basilica) 大肆售券。1517年10月，馬丁路德 (Martin Luther) 在威登堡教堂大門貼出《九十五點論證》：
The Church sold indulgences to build St. Peter's Basilica. In Oct 1517, Martin Luther posted the Ninety-Five Theses on the Wittenberg church door:

  • 主張「因信稱義」(Salvation by faith alone)，買贖罪券無法確保靈魂得救。
    Advocated "Salvation by faith alone", buying indulgences cannot guarantee salvation.
  • 強調唯有上帝能赦免罪惡，教宗無權決定靈魂去向。
    Emphasised only God can forgive sins, the Pope has no authority over souls.
  • 這份論證迅速被翻譯並藉由印刷術傳遍全歐洲。
    The theses were quickly translated and printed across Europe.

新教派別興起 Rise of Protestant Churches

1521年路德被逐出天主教會，但在德意志貴族支持下新教快速發展：
Excommunicated in 1521, Luther gained noble support, leading to new churches:

  • 路德宗 Lutheranism: 德國及北歐主流教派。(Mainstream in Germany & Northern Europe)
  • 歸正宗 Reformed Church: 由加爾文 (Calvin) 在瑞士推動，主張預定論。(Pushed by Calvin in Switzerland, taught Predestination)
  • 聖公宗 Anglican Church: 英國國王亨利八世因離婚爭端創立。(Established by King Henry VIII of England)

天主教會反宗教改革 Counter-Reformation

天主教會內部推動改革以挽回信徒與聲譽：
Internal reform by the Catholic Church to regain followers and reputation:

  • 全面禁止販售贖罪券，成立耶穌會 (Society of Jesus) 專責傳教與教育。(Banned indulgence sales, formed Society of Jesus for education/mission)
  • 給予天主教國家君主更多宗教事務自主權。(Granted Catholic monarchs more religious autonomy)
  • 堅持唯有天主教會擁有詮釋聖經的權威。(Maintained that Church alone has authority to interpret the Bible)

自由民主 Freedom & Democracy 提倡個人信仰自由與「上帝面前人人平等」，奠定了歐洲現代自由民主思想的基礎。 Promoted individual freedom of faith and equality before God, laying the foundations for modern European democratic thought.
性質 Nature: 一場科學與科技運動
A scientific and technological movement
發源地 Birthplace: 歐洲
Europe
時期 Period: 17世紀
17th century

三項成因 Causes:

(i) 文藝復興與印刷術 Influence of Renaissance & Printing Press

文藝復興期間歐洲人提出新科學理論。印刷術 (printing press) 促進書籍流通並推動學者間的交流。
Europeans proposed new theories. Printing press promoted book circulation and scholarly exchanges.

(ii) 歐洲君主的支持 Support from European Kings

自文藝復興起科學挑戰教會權威。強大的君主資助科學研究，以削弱教會並加強自身權力與國家實力。
Strong kings funded science to weaken the Church and strengthen their own power.

(iii) 新航路開闢的鼓舞 Impact of European Exploration

15-16世紀開闢新航路 (Opening of New Sea Routes)，環球航行證實地球是圓的並推翻舊理論，鼓舞歐洲人探索科學領域。
Circumnavigation proved Earth is round, encouraging exploration in science.

主要發展 Major Developments:

(i) 科學學會的成立 Formation of Scientific Societies

過往科學工作多由教會主導且很少分享；17世紀出現獨立科學學會（如1660年成立的「皇家學會 Royal Society」），舉行會議並出版期刊，提供學術交流平台。
Independent societies (e.g. Royal Society in 1660) held meetings and published journals for academic exchanges.

(ii) 科學方法與主要成就 Scientific Methods & Main Achievements

學者強調「科學方法」(scientific methods)：需要懷疑精神 (scepticism)、觀察 (observation)、假設 (hypothesis)、實驗 (experimentation) 及理性分析 (rational analysis) 以得出結論。
Stressed scientific methods: scepticism, observation, hypothesis, experiment, and rational analysis.

牛頓 Newton (1642-1727): 提出「萬有引力定律」(law of universal gravitation)。
Suggested the law of universal gravitation.

影響 Impacts:

(i) 啟發歐洲人的新思維 Inspiring New European Mindset

推動理性思考與質疑精神，深化對自然的理解。「自然法則」(laws of nature) 啟發思想家將其應用於政治與社會，引發17世紀末的啟蒙運動。
Encouraged rational thinking. "Laws of nature" inspired thinkers to apply principles to politics, sparking the Enlightenment.

(ii) 奠定近代歐洲科技優勢 Foundation for Technological Supremacy

科學家發現自然法則並應用於工業與交通，新科技快速湧現，為18世紀工業革命 (Industrial Revolution) 奠定基礎。
Natural laws applied to industry laid the foundation for the Industrial Revolution.

性質 Nature: 一場思想與文化運動
An intellectual and cultural movement
發源地 Birthplace: 歐洲
Europe
時期 Period: 17世紀中至18世紀
mid-17th and 18th centuries
核心主張 Core Ideas:

啟蒙思想家提倡人人享有「天賦人權」(natural rights) 如自由 (liberty) 與平等 (equality)，反對政治與社會不公義，成為近代歐洲政治史的里程碑。
Advocated that everyone enjoys natural rights (liberty, equality) and opposed political/social injustice.

兩大背景 Background:

(i) 歐洲人價值觀的轉變 Changes in European Values

文藝復興、宗教改革與科學革命推動人文主義與理性思考，挑戰傳統權威，並開始將這些價值應用於政治與社會。
Promoted humanism and rational thinking, applying values to politics and society.

(ii) 對專制主義的不滿 Discontent with Absolutism

16-17世紀國王權力擴張形成專制主義 (absolutism)，專制國王握有無限制權力並打壓反對者。他們支持「君權神授」(Divine Right of Kings)，宣稱權力來自上帝。17世紀中葉起歐洲人開始批評專制主義。
Absolute kings with unlimited power supported Divine Right of Kings. People challenged absolutism for violating rights.

主要啟蒙思想家 Major Thinkers:

洛克 Locke (1632-1704)

天賦人權 Natural Rights: 人生而擁有生命、自由與財產權 (life, liberty, property)。
社會契約 Social Contract: 政府由人民與統治者訂立契約建立，若政府侵害權利，人民有權推翻。
Governments established by social contract; people can overthrow government violating rights.

孟德斯鳩 Montesquieu (1689-1755)

三權分立 Separation of Powers: 提出立法、行政與司法權力分立 (legislative, executive, judicial powers)。
權力應互相制衡 (checks and balances)，防止專制與濫權。
Separated powers into 3 branches to check each other and prevent absolutism.

盧梭 Rousseau (1712-1778)

主權在民 Popular Sovereignty: 政府權力來自人民，統治者應依據大眾意願 (general will) 治理。
若統治者違背民意，人民有權廢除。
Government power comes from the people and must follow the general will.

歷史影響 Historical Impacts:

1. 激發革命與政治改革 Sparked Revolutions & Reforms: 啟蒙思想削弱了「君權神授」，啟發了美國獨立戰爭 (American Revolution, 1775) 與法國大革命 (French Revolution, 1789)。
Weakened Divine Right of Kings, inspiring American and French Revolutions.

2. 建立現代憲政體制 Established Modern Constitutions: 許多國家制定憲法 (constitutions)，實行三權分立與保障天賦人權，奠定現代民主制度。
Countries created constitutions implementing separation of powers to safeguard rights.

拜占庭帝國滅亡；歐洲人進行海上探索環球航行，證實地球是圓的。
Fall of Byzantine Empire; sea routes exploration proved the Earth is round.

馬丁路德張貼《九十五點論證》，基督教分裂，新教派別成立。

強調科學方法與自然法則，牛頓提出萬有引力定律。

Mid 17th - 18th Century (17世紀中至18世紀)

Promoted natural rights, social contract, and separation of powers against absolutism.

18th - 19th Century

科學與啟蒙思想實踐，歐洲全面邁入近代現代化社會。
Scientific and Enlightenment ideas put to practice, leading to modern society.
